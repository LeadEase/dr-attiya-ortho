## What this project is

This is not intended to be a normal dental-practice website. It is a focused, clinician-led landing page for Dr Attiya Ahmed at NuDental/Nu Dentalign. Its main purpose is to receive high-intent visitors from Google Search Ads and, later, Meta advertising, then convert those visitors into suitable clear-aligner assessment enquiries.

The desired journey is:

**Google or Meta advert → Dr Attiya landing page → short two-step enquiry → NuDental receives the completed enquiry → team calls the patient → assessment is arranged → patient attends → treatment plan is discussed.**

The commercial objective is not to generate the largest possible number of cheap form fills. It is to generate people who understand the basic offer, are genuinely considering teeth straightening, can be contacted, and are more likely to book and attend a full smile assessment. Judge the page by qualified assessment bookings, attended assessments and eventual orthodontic treatment starts—not by clicks or Step 1 completions alone.

The page must feel like the beginning of a professional clinical relationship with Dr Attiya, not a discount funnel, a generic dental template or a pushy sales consultation.

## The central proposition

The strongest reason to enquire is not simply that aligners start from £1,999. It is that the patient can meet Dr Attiya for a thorough £25 Full Smile Assessment, understand their teeth and bite, see the available options and receive a personalised direction before committing to treatment.

The assessment should be presented as including, subject to the clinical pathway being appropriate:

1. A careful assessment of the patient’s smile, teeth, bite and oral health.
2. An iTero 3D digital scan.
3. A digital smile simulation or preview where appropriate. This is illustrative and must never be presented as a guaranteed final result.
4. An explanation of potentially suitable treatment options, including clear aligners and fixed braces.
5. A personalised treatment estimate.
6. An explanation of the likely treatment journey.
7. An indicative treatment duration.
8. Small dental X-rays only where clinically indicated—not automatically for every patient.

Keep this explanation concise and visually clear. Patients should understand the value of the appointment within seconds without reading a wall of clinical text.

The preferred assessment message is simply **“Full Smile Assessment — £25.”** Do not reintroduce “Usually £150, now £25” or a crossed-out reference price unless NuDental provides evidence that £150 is a genuine established usual selling price and specifically approves the promotion.

## How the page should feel

The visual direction is premium, calm, personal and trustworthy. Use NuDental’s black, warm gold and white as the core palette, softened with warm ivory, charcoal and neutral backgrounds so the page does not feel harsh or overly black.

The page should be:

- led by real photography of Dr Attiya;
- warm and clinician-led rather than corporate;
- image-rich but not crowded;
- easy to scan;
- written in simple British English;
- understandable to a patient with no dental knowledge;
- polished on mobile first, then desktop;
- quick to load;
- crisp in Safari as well as Chrome;
- accessible by keyboard and usable with reduced motion;
- restrained in animation and decorative effects.

Avoid generic AI phrases, vague superlatives, huge blocks of text, complicated terminology, artificial scarcity, countdowns, aggressive pop-ups and stock-heavy “perfect smile” clichés. Do not make the page feel as though it is aimed at other dentists. It is for ordinary patients who may feel uncertain, self-conscious or nervous and want a clear next step.

## Form and enquiry journey

The enquiry flow is one of the most important parts of the project. Keep it extremely simple.

### Step 1 — contact details

Collect exactly:

- Full name — required
- Phone number — required
- Email address — required

The button is **Continue**. Do not add budget, preferred appointment time, date of birth, treatment history or a free-text medical question. Do not send an email or treat the person as a completed lead merely because they move to Step 2.

Use a short, plain privacy line explaining that by continuing/submitting, the person is asking NuDental to call or email them about the enquiry, with a direct link to the privacy notice. Avoid lengthy legal copy in the visible form.

### Step 2 — price awareness

Show:

**Clear Aligner Treatment From £1,999**

Explain briefly that the final price depends on clinical suitability and the personalised treatment plan, and that the £25 assessment is separate.

Ask exactly:

**“Are you aware that clear aligner treatment starts from £1,999?”**

Answers:

- Yes
- No

Accept both answers. Never reject someone for selecting No. Mark No leads so the NuDental team knows that pricing needs to be explained. The final button is **Confirm My Enquiry**.

Only after the completed final submission is accepted by the email provider should the interface show success and count the enquiry as a lead. No payment is taken through the page. The success message should be brief: NuDental has received the enquiry and will make contact.

The form must remain on the same page. All primary CTAs should scroll the visitor back to the first form and focus the first field. On mobile, retain a sticky call/form action that does not obscure content.

## Writing standard

Write for patients, not clinicians or developers. Aim for language that a ten-year-old could follow without making it childish. Use short sentences, familiar words and direct answers.

Avoid internal phrases such as:

- “landing-page offer”;
- “the supplied finance illustration shows”;
- “lead qualification mechanism”;
- “conversion architecture”;
- “clinically predictable perfection”.

Patient copy should sound more like:

- “See what may suit your smile.”
- “Understand your options before you decide.”
- “Change the treatment total to see example monthly payments.”
- “The NuDental team will call you to discuss the next step.”

Keep required clinical qualifications and disclaimers, but integrate them quietly. Do not let compliance wording dominate the page.

## Analytics and conversion intent

Preserve campaign parameters and the existing non-personal events, but never send names, phone numbers or email addresses to analytics. Do not allow tracking scripts to load before the visitor’s relevant consent choice.

Do not turn the visible page into an SEO article and do not stuff locations or repetitive keywords into patient copy. The conversion experience remains the priority.
